Manufacturing, connected work & customer experience

Manufacturing is a core interest in my work. I like exploring the points where people, systems, and customer commitments meet.

Technology becomes easier to discuss when the business problem is tangible. I create short, AI-assisted animations around illustrative manufacturing scenarios to help start those conversations.

When an order becomes a customer promise

A distributor needs stock before a promotion, but a pricing mismatch holds the order. My CRM animation explores the handoffs between customer service, sales operations, and fulfilment. The question behind the story is simple: who can see the whole issue, and who owns the next step?

Watch my manufacturing and CRM animation on LinkedIn.

When a line stops, the response matters

A single equipment fault can affect production, dispatch, and the customer’s shelf. My factory-floor animation explores the value of shared context across maintenance, IT, and production. Engineers lead the repair; coordination helps the wider business respond to its consequences.

Watch my factory-floor animation on LinkedIn.

A starting point for discovery

Where does work get stuck between your teams? Which handoff depends on chasing an update? What does that delay mean for a customer? These questions make a technology discussion more grounded in the work people actually do.

These animations are illustrative scenarios, not records of customer implementations or promises of specific results.
