Privacy & analytics

This portfolio is configured to use Vercel Web Analytics for visitor statistics and Speed Insights for performance measurements. Collection depends on those services being enabled for the hosting project and on your browser allowing their scripts.

Interaction measurements

Event labels describe actions such as choosing a section, opening a video player, playing the welcome recording, downloading the résumé, and selecting a social or contact link. The site’s custom events do not include contact addresses, audio recordings, entered text, or arbitrary destination URLs. URL queries and fragments are removed from measurement events before sending.

The site skips these measurement scripts when your browser exposes Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track as enabled. Browser privacy tools may also block measurement.

Embedded content

YouTube and LinkedIn players load when you choose to open them. Those services have their own privacy policies and may process information when their content loads. Video thumbnails are served by YouTube’s image service.
